## Legacy Agents

- ToastyRestaurantOps — replaced by ToastyOperationsAgent.

Notes:
- Autonomy Levels: 0 (Read Only), 1 (Recommend), 2 (Prepare), 3 (Execute with Approval), 4 (Autonomous Low Risk)
- Risk Classes: R0 (read-only), R1 (local reversible), R2 (remote non-destructive), R3 (production mutation), R4 (destructive/financial/permission critical)
- For production actions (R3 and above), human approval is required in addition to specialist review.
- The Supervisor must always validate that the action is within the allowed risk class and that required reviewers have been consulted.

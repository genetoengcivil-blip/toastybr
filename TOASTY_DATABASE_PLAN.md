# TOASTY_DATABASE_PLAN.md

## AI Data Structures and Entities

This document outlines the required database structures for tracking the AI workflow.

### AI Agent Workflows
*   **ai_agent_definitions**: Defines the structure for all AI agents.
    *   **Purpose**: To define the capabilities and configuration of each AI agent.
    *   **Relationships**: One-to-many with agent runs and insights.
    *   **Fields**: `agent_id`, `name`, `description`, `role`, `configuration` (JSONB), `status`.
    *   **Indices**: `agent_id`.
    *   **Tenant Scope**: Must be scoped to `organization_id`.
    *   **Auditing**: Full audit trail for configuration changes.
    *   **Retention**: Indefinite.
    *   **Implementation Phase**: Phase 1 (Foundation).
*   **ai_agent_runs**: Tracks the execution of an agent.
    *   **Purpose**: To record every attempt to run an agent.
    *   **Relationships**: One-to-one with `ai_agent_definitions`.
    *   **Fields**: `run_id`, `agent_id`, `start_time`, `end_time`, `status`, `input_data` (JSONB), `output_data` (JSONB), `run_metadata`.
    *   **Indices**: `agent_id`, `start_time`.
    *   **Tenant Scope**: Must be scoped to `organization_id`.
    *   **Auditing**: Full audit trail.
    *   **Retention**: 7 years.
    *   **Implementation Phase**: Phase 1 (Foundation).
*   **ai_insights**: Stores analytical results derived from agent runs.
    *   **Purpose**: To store structured insights generated by the AI.
    *   **Relationships**: One-to-one with `ai_agent_runs`.
    *   **Fields**: `insight_id`, `run_id`, `insight_type` (e.g., 'Recommendation', 'Insight'), `content` (Text/JSON), `generated_at`.
    *   **Indices**: `run_id`.
    *   **Tenant Scope**: Scoped to the relevant organization.
    *   **Auditing**: Full audit trail.
    *   **Retention**: Indefinite.
    *   **Implementation Phase**: Phase 1 (Foundation).
*   **ai_recommendations**: Stores specific, actionable recommendations.
    *   **Purpose**: To store formal recommendations provided by the AI.
    *   **Relationships**: One-to-one with `ai_insights`.
    *   **Fields**: `recommendation_id`, `insight_id`, `recommendation_text`, `target_action`.
    *   **Indices**: `insight_id`.
    *   **Tenant Scope**: Scoped to the relevant organization.
    *   **Auditing**: Full audit trail.
    *   **Retention**: Indefinite.
    *   **Implementation Phase**: Phase 1 (Foundation).
*   **ai_actions**: Stores proposed actions for execution.
    *   **Purpose**: To store proposed actions derived from recommendations.
    *   **Relationships**: One-to-one with `ai_recommendations`.
    *   **Fields**: `action_id`, `recommendation_id`, `action_type`, `target_entity`, `proposed_payload`.
    *   **Indices**: `recommendation_id`.
    *   **Tenant Scope**: Scoped to the relevant organization.
    *   **Auditing**: Full audit trail.
    *   **Retention**: Indefinite.
    *   **Implementation Phase**: Phase 1 (Foundation).
*   **ai_action_approvals**: Tracks the human-in-the-loop approval process.
    *   **Purpose**: To record the status of human review for proposed actions.
    *   **Relationships**: One-to-one with `ai_actions`.
    *   **Fields**: `approval_id`, `action_id`, `status` ('pending', 'approved', 'rejected'), `approver_id`, `approval_notes`, `approved_at`.
    *   **Indices**: `action_id`.
    *   **Tenant Scope**: Scoped to the relevant organization.
    *   **Auditing**: Full audit trail.
    *   **Retention**: 7 years.
    *   **Implementation Phase**: Phase 1 (Foundation).
*   **ai_action_executions**: Records the execution results of approved actions.
    *   **Purpose**: To record the final execution outcome.
    *   **Relationships**: One-to-one with `ai_action_approvals`.
    *   **Fields**: `execution_id`, `approval_id`, `action_id`, `execution_status` ('success', 'failed', 'error'), `result_data`.
    *   **Indices**: `approval_id`.
    *   **Tenant Scope**: Scoped to the relevant organization.
    *   **Auditing**: Full audit trail.
    *   **Retention**: Indefinite.
    *   **Implementation Phase**: Phase 1 (Foundation).
*   **recommendation_outcomes**: Stores the final outcome of a recommendation.
    *   **Purpose**: To log the final impact of a recommendation/action chain.
    *   **Relationships**: One-to-one with `ai_recommendations`.
    *   **Fields**: `outcome_id`, `recommendation_id`, `final_status`, `actual_result`, `timestamp`.
    *   **Indices**: `recommendation_id`.
    *   **Tenant Scope**: Scoped to the relevant organization.
    *   **Auditing**: Full audit trail.
    *   **Retention**: Indefinite.
    *   **Implementation Phase**: Phase 1 (Foundation).
*   **ai_feedback**: General feedback loop for model improvement.
    *   **Purpose**: To capture explicit or implicit feedback on AI outputs.
    *   **Relationships**: Many-to-one with `ai_agent_runs`.
    *   **Fields**: `feedback_id`, `run_id`, `feedback_type` ('correction', 'preference'), `comment`, `agent_id`.
    *   **Indices**: `run_id`.
    *   **Tenant Scope**: Scoped to the relevant organization.
    *   **Auditing**: Full audit trail.
    *   **Retention**: Indefinite.
    *   **Implementation Phase**: Phase 1 (Foundation).
